Our Story

Teranga Kitchen was founded in 2015 by Chef Fatou Jallof, who grew up cooking traditional dishes with her grandmother in a small village in Senegal.

After years of perfecting her craft, she opened Teranga Kitchen to share the authentic flavors of West Africa with everyone. Today, we serve hundreds of happy customers every week.

What We Offer

  • Authentic Gambia and senegal cuisine
  • Fresh, Locally-soured ingredients
  • Family-friendly atmosphere
  • Catering services for events
  • Takeaway and delivery options
